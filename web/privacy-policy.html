Bloomy Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 28, 2026

Bloomy ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Bloomy mobile app and web experience.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information such as email, username, and display name.
  • User content you create, including posts, pins, journal entries, and mood logs.
  • Basic technical data required to operate and secure the app.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide core app features and personalize your experience.
  • To maintain account security and prevent misuse.
  • To improve app quality, reliability, and performance.

3. Storage and Security

Bloomy uses Firebase services for authentication and data storage. We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared only with service providers necessary to run the app (for example, hosting and storage providers) or when required by law.

5. Your Controls

  • You can edit profile information inside the app.
  • You can control profile visibility using public/private settings.
  • You can request account/content removal by contacting support.

6. Children's Privacy

Bloomy is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). If we learn we collected data from a child without required consent, we will remove it.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact: bloomy.app.help@gmail.com
